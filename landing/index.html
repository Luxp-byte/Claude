Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Download the file and open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage on your device only. Use "Export backup" regularly. If you clear your browser data, it will be erased unless you have a backup.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes. Export a backup on one and import it on the other. There is no automatic sync.

Does it calculate my taxes?

It adds a sales tax/VAT/GST line to invoices at the rate you set and totals your expenses. It is not tax or accounting advice. Check the rules in your country with a professional.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once.
