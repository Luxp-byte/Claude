FREELANCER INVOICE & EXPENSE KIT: QUICK START

1. Save "invoice-kit.html" somewhere permanent (for example, Documents).
   Always open it from the same place and in the same browser, because your
   data is stored by the browser for that file location.

2. Double-click the file. It opens in your browser.

3. Go to Settings and enter your business name, address, currency
   (e.g. USD, EUR, GBP, INR), tax label and rate, and payment details.
   Upload your logo if you have one.

4. Add your clients under Clients.

5. Under Invoices, click "+ New invoice", add line items, and Save.
   Click "PDF" (or "Preview / PDF") and choose "Save as PDF" as the
   printer to get a PDF to email your client.

6. When you're paid, click "Mark paid". Sent invoices past their due date
   are flagged as overdue automatically.

7. Log business costs under Expenses. Use "Export CSV" at year end.

BACK UP YOUR DATA
Settings → "Export backup" saves everything to a .json file. Do this
regularly. Clearing your browser history/site data will erase the app's
data. To move to a new computer, export there and import here.

Want to look around first? On the Dashboard, click "load sample data".
Use "Erase all data" in Settings when you're ready to start for real.
